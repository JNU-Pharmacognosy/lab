Welcome to Our Lab

We are a pharmacognosy and analytical chemistry research group in the College of Pharmacy at Jeju National University, South Korea.

Our work centers on the phytochemical profiling and bioactivity evaluation of native and endemic plants of Jeju Island — a volcanic island recognized as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve for its exceptional botanical diversity.

A recurring goal of our research is identifying botanical substitutes for scarce or endangered crude drug resources, supported by rigorous analytical method validation and chemometric analysis.

We train and mentor graduate students at the master's and doctoral level, with hands-on guidance through manuscript preparation and international peer review.

Research Focus Areas

Four pillars connect natural product discovery to rigorous, publishable science.

🌿

Pharmacognosy & Substitute Crude Drug Discovery

Screening Jeju's endemic and native plants as sustainable alternatives to scarce pharmacopoeial crude drugs.

🔬

LC-MS Phytochemical Profiling

UHPLC-PDA and LC-DAD-QToF-MS profiling to characterize and annotate plant secondary metabolites.

📈

Analytical Method Validation

Method development and validation following ICH Q2(R1)/Q2(R2) guidelines for regulatory-grade data.

🧬

Bioactivity-Guided Evaluation

Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial assays linking chemical profiles to biological function.

A Living Laboratory: Jeju Island

  • UNESCO Biosphere Reserve with exceptional botanical diversity, shaped by its volcanic geology and subtropical-to-alpine climate gradient.
  • Our group completed a 3-year field survey covering 798 plant species, including congeneric pharmacopoeial analysis of Jeju's medicinal flora.
  • Endemic species such as Empetrum nigrum var. japonicum and Centella asiatica offer research questions rarely accessible elsewhere.
  • A compact island setting puts field collection, extraction, and instrumentation minutes apart from campus and coastline alike.
